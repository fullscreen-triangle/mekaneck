\chapter{Consciousness Dynamics During Athletic Performance}
\label{ch:athletic_validation}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{Cardiac-Referenced Hierarchical Phase Synchronization} (experimental validation sections) --- \texttt{docs/perception/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
A 400-metre sprint provides the most direct empirical window into real-time consciousness dynamics available in the existing literature: the combination of extreme physical demand, measurable performance output, controlled environmental conditions, and continuous physiological monitoring creates a natural experimental protocol for testing the categorical geometry framework. This chapter presents the full athletic performance dataset, the detection of 600--900 individual thoughts during a single sprint through oscillatory hole completion events, the validation of the 2.345~Hz perception rate, and the cardiac-referenced horizon chart methodology that visualises consciousness geometry in real time.
\end{chapterabstract}
