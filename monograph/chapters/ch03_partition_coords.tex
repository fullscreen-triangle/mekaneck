\chapter{Partition Coordinates in Bounded Phase Space}
\label{ch:partition_coords}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{Partition Coordinate Geometry in Bounded Oscillatory Systems} --- \texttt{docs/bounded-systems/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
We develop the partition coordinate system $(n, \ell, m, s)$ from first principles, deriving it from the geometry of nested boundary constraints in bounded oscillatory phase spaces. The capacity theorem establishes that the maximum number of distinct categorical states at partition depth $n$ is exactly $2n^2$---a result that will recur throughout the monograph as the fundamental counting constraint on consciousness. Partition signatures are shown to uniquely characterise molecular configurations, enabling the identification and design of compounds through their categorical rather than spatial properties.
\end{chapterabstract}
