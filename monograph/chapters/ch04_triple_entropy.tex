\chapter{Triple Entropy Equivalence: $S_{\mathrm{osc}} = S_{\mathrm{cat}} = S_{\mathrm{part}}$}
\label{ch:triple_entropy}

\papernote{Source papers: \emph{Unified Entropy from Oscillation, Category, and Partition} --- \texttt{blindhorse/docs/categorical-partitioning/}; and \emph{Integrated Measurement Framework} --- \texttt{blindhorse/docs/hybrid-fluidic-circuit-dynamics/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Three independent derivations---one from oscillatory mechanics, one from categorical state counting, one from partition geometry---converge on the identical entropy formula $S = \kB M \ln n$. This triple equivalence is the central mathematical result of the monograph. It proves that oscillatory dynamics, categorical enumeration, and geometric partitioning are not three different descriptions of three different things, but three observational perspectives on a single underlying structure. The theorem establishes partition operations as thermodynamically irreversible, resolving the origin of the entropy arrow within the categorical framework.
\end{chapterabstract}
