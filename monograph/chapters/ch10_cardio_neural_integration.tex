\chapter{Hybrid Microfluidic Circuit Dynamics}
\label{ch:hybrid_circuits}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{Integrated Measurement Framework for Molecular Configuration State Dynamics} --- \texttt{blindhorse/docs/hybrid-fluidic-circuit-dynamics/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Biological systems are hybrid microfluidic analog oscillatory integrated circuits. This chapter develops the unified theory of oscillatory, categorical, and partitioning information processing within such circuits, with experimental validation through four independent measurement modalities: vibrational spectroscopy, electromagnetic field mapping, dielectric response analysis, and gas molecular tracking. The $10^{22}$-fold efficiency gain of geometric pattern operation versus microstate enumeration is proven and experimentally confirmed.
\end{chapterabstract}
