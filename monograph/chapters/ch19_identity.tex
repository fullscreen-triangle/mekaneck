\chapter{Identity, Naming Circuits, and the Self}
\label{ch:identity}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{Charge Distribution Dynamics in Closed Hybrid Microfluidic Circuits} --- \texttt{docs/charge-coupled-hybrid-microfluidic-systems/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
In a closed charge-coupled circuit with no external ground, charge redistribution is autocatalytic and perpetual oscillation is a thermodynamic necessity. Self-identity emerges through meta-recognition of naming patterns in this non-grounded circuit. The mathematical isomorphism between physical charge circuits and naming circuits is established: the same equations govern both. Consciousness is then characterised as a non-grounded naming circuit, explaining why the self cannot step outside itself to observe itself objectively (there is no external ground) and why identity is stable despite continuous change (the attractor dynamics of charge balance).
\end{chapterabstract}
