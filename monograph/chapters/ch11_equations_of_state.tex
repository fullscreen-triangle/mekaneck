\chapter{Equations of State for Biological Oscillatory Circuits}
\label{ch:equations_of_state}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{Partition-Based Equations of State for Hybrid Microfluidic Circuits} --- \texttt{docs/equations-of-state-hybrid-circuits/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
A complete set of equations of state for biological oscillatory circuits is derived from three foundational axioms: bounded phase space, finite observational resolution, and the No Null State Principle. The triple equivalence theorem $\tripleequiv$ emerges as the equations of state for any such circuit. Five operational regimes are characterised---coherent, turbulent, hierarchical cascade, aperture-dominated, and phase-locked---with the conditions for transitions between them. Circuit state is shown to be uniquely determined by twelve coupled coordinate systems. Oscillation arises as a consequence of categorical necessity, not of forces.
\end{chapterabstract}
