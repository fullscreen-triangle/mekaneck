\chapter{Categorical Dynamics of Biological Maxwell Demons}
\label{ch:biological_maxwell}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{Categorical Dynamics of Maxwell Demons} --- \texttt{docs/categories/biological-maxwell-demons/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Biological Maxwell Demons (\BMD{}s) are the operational units of consciousness: they are oscillatory holes being filled, information catalysts in which ``many things'' means the multi-valued selection from $\sim 10^6$ weak-force completions. This chapter develops the full categorical dynamics of \BMD{}s from first principles, establishing their role as the interface between the thermodynamic substrate (Part~III) and the phenomenological structure (Part~IV). Eight structural properties of \BMD{}s are derived and validated across scales from individual enzymes to neural circuits.
\end{chapterabstract}
