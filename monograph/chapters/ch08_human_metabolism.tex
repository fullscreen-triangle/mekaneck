\begin{chapterabstract}
Molecular oxygen (\Otwo) is not merely a metabolic fuel but the universal information substrate of biological systems. With 25,110 accessible quantum states and an 8,000-fold oscillatory information density enhancement relative to nitrogen, oxygen molecules serve as the ``categorical clocks'' swimming in the \Hplus soup. This chapter establishes the oxygen quantum state manifold, the atmospheric coupling mechanism that transmits environmental oscillatory information into the cellular interior, and the proof that biological information processing requires oxygen not only energetically but informationally.
\end{chapterabstract}
