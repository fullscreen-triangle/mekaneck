\chapter{Resolution of Gibbs' Paradox Through Categorical Irreversibility}
\label{ch:gibbs}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{On the Consequences of Categorical Completion in Gas Dynamics} --- \texttt{docs/gibbs-paradox/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Gibbs' paradox---the discontinuous entropy change upon mixing of identical gases---is resolved by recognising that physical states are distinguished not by spatial arrangement alone but by position in an irreversible categorical completion sequence. Once a state is completed, it cannot be re-occupied: categorical irreversibility necessitates entropy increase even for macroscopically identical configurations. The microscopic mechanism is phase-lock network densification. This resolution demonstrates that the partition coordinate framework is not merely an alternative description of statistical mechanics but makes predictions that classical statistical mechanics cannot.
\end{chapterabstract}
