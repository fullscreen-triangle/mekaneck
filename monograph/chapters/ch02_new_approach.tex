\begin{chapterabstract}
This chapter previews the categorical geometric resolution of the hard problem. Consciousness is a derived physical process, not an additional primitive property. The process is categorical completion: the closure of oscillatory circuits in the proton electric field substrate. Three central claims are stated and mapped to the chapters that prove them: (1) circuit completion is physically mandatory for fast-responding organisms; (2) consciousness geometry is measurable by proxy through the confluence of perception flux and thought geometry; (3) the triple entropy equivalence $\tripleequiv$ provides the unifying mathematical structure across all scales.
\end{chapterabstract}
