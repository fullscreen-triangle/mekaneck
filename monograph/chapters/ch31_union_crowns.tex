\chapter{Classical-Quantum Equivalence: The Union of Two Crowns}
\label{ch:union_crowns}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{On the Consequences of Partitioning Mechanisms in Oscillatory Categorical Dynamics} --- \texttt{docs/union-of-two-crowns/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Classical and quantum mechanics are mathematically equivalent descriptions of partition geometry in bounded phase spaces. This is the ``Union of Two Crowns'': the two theoretical frameworks are not competing descriptions of different regimes but identical descriptions of the same geometry from two observational perspectives. The unification is established through the triple equivalence: oscillatory dynamics, categorical enumeration, and partition operations yield $S = \kB M \ln n$ through independent routes. Mass is the unifying quantity: quantum state occupation equals the classical dynamics equation. Mass spectrometry and chromatographic separation provide experimental validation of the cross-framework predictions.
\end{chapterabstract}
