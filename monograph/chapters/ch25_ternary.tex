\chapter{Ternary Representation of S-Entropy Space}
\label{ch:ternary}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{On the Consequences of Categorical Mechanics on Computing Datum Encoding: Ternary Unit Representation} --- \texttt{docs/ternary-representation/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
S-entropy space is three-dimensional: $[0,1]^3$ with knowledge ($S_k$), temporal ($S_t$), and evolution ($S_e$) axes. Binary encoding is structurally incapable of representing this space efficiently: each bit addresses a one-dimensional projection. Ternary (base-3) encoding is the natural language of three-dimensional state space. The trit-coordinate correspondence theorem establishes a bijection: a $k$-trit string addresses exactly one cell in the $3^k$ hierarchical partition of $S$-entropy space. An infinite ternary string converges to a unique point in the continuum. Hardware mapping to three-phase oscillators is constructed explicitly.
\end{chapterabstract}
