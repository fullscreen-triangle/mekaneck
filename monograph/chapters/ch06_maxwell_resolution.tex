\chapter{The Resolution of Maxwell's Demon}
\label{ch:maxwell_resolution}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{On the Resolution of Maxwell's Demon} --- \texttt{mekaneck/docs/resolution/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Maxwell's demon paradox is dissolved through phase-lock network theory. There is no demon because there is no velocity-based sorting: molecules exist in networks formed by van der Waals and dipole interactions, and network membership is independent of kinetic energy. Six independent proofs establish temporal triviality, temperature independence, the retrieval paradox, kinetic independence, and categorical-physical distance inequivalence. The information complementarity theorem establishes that kinetic and categorical information are orthogonal faces of the same molecular state, accessible to different classes of observers.
\end{chapterabstract}
