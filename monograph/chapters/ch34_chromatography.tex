\chapter{S-Distance Chromatography}
\label{ch:chromatography}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{S-Distance Chromatography: A Geometric Framework for Retention Prediction and Virtual Column Switching} --- \texttt{mekaneck/docs/mass-transfer/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Chromatographic retention is a geometric property of S-entropy space, not a property of molecular structure per se. The S-distance metric $d_S(A,B)$ completely characterises retention behaviour across all separation mechanisms. Mechanism-specific weight vectors form an orthogonal basis, enabling virtual column switching through coordinate transformations: retention on any column can be predicted from measurements on any other, with 5.4\% mean absolute error across 1,847~compound-column pairs. The first-principles resolution formula derived here supersedes the empirical van Deemter equation and reveals its approximation regime explicitly.
\end{chapterabstract}
