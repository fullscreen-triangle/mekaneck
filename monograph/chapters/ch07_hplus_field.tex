\chapter{The \texorpdfstring{H\textsuperscript{+}}{H+} Electric Field: The Reality Substrate}
\label{ch:hplus_field}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{On the Consequences of Categorical Completion on Cognitive Processes} --- \texttt{docs/categorical-completion/} (substrate sections)}

\begin{chapterabstract}
The physical substrate of consciousness is identified as the dynamic electric field generated by proton (\Hplus) flux at $\sim 10^{13}$~Hz. This field is the ``unperceivable reality substrate''---it oscillates too rapidly to be experienced directly, and so is experienced as the unchanging background of reality. The definition of a categorical state as an element of an ordered completion sequence within this field is formalised, and the time-as-categorical-assignment-rate theorem is proven: subjective time is the felt rate at which new categorical states are assigned in the \Hplus field, not thermodynamic entropy flow.
\end{chapterabstract}
