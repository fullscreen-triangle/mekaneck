\chapter{The Experience of Time}
\label{ch:time}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{On the Consequences of Duration in Circuit Completion: A Mechanistic Synthesis of Time as Geometric Progression Experience} --- \texttt{mekaneck/docs/geometric\_experience\_of\_time/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Subjective time is the felt experience of geometric tracing during circuit completion events. The block universe (the timeless mathematical structure of spacetime) is fully compatible with the phenomenology of temporal flow: flow arises because physical instantiation of mathematical structure requires temporal tracing, and tracing takes time. Internal time is defined as $T_{\mathrm{internal}} = \sum_i \tau_{\mathrm{circuit}}(i)$, the sum of individual circuit completion durations. The specious present ($\sim$100--1,000~ms) is derived as the duration of an oscillatory hole ensemble completion. Temporal elasticity---the subjective acceleration and deceleration of time during extreme states---is explained by changes in hole-filling rate. Three independent experimental validations are presented.
\end{chapterabstract}
