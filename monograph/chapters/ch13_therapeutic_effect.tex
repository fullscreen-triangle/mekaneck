\begin{chapterabstract}
This chapter presents the complete solution to the hard problem of consciousness. Conscious experience is the continuous completion of oscillatory circuits in the \Hplus electric field substrate. The three-level architecture (\Hplus $\to$ \Otwo $\to$ consciousness) is developed in full: the proton field creates the unperceivable substrate, oxygen traversal creates oscillatory holes, neural completion of those holes \emph{is} experience. Olfaction is treated as the canonical validation case: the smell of a molecule is not a representation of the molecule but the circuit closure event itself. Thought is internally generated olfaction. The hard problem does not require new physics; it requires recognising that the explanatory gap closes when one identifies circuit completion as the missing process.
\end{chapterabstract}
