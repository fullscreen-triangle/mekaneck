\chapter{The Metabolic Cost of Consciousness}
\label{ch:metabolic_cost}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{The Metabolic Cost of Consciousness} --- \texttt{docs/thought-metabolism/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Consciousness costs approximately 30~W: $\sim$20~W for coherent thought formation and $\sim$11~W for perception integration, totalling $287 \pm 94$~kcal/day for thought and $156 \pm 48$~kcal/day for perception. These figures are isolated through the activity-sleep mirror method: daytime categorical error accumulation must exactly match the nighttime cleanup capacity, providing a tight constraint on the energy budget of consciousness. The oxygen-consciousness energy scaling theorem establishes why atmospheric oxygen concentration is the binding constraint on cognitive energy expenditure. The framework predicts specific metabolic signatures distinguishing conscious from unconscious processing that are validated against published positron emission tomography data.
\end{chapterabstract}
