\chapter{Hardware-Based Temporal Measurements}
\label{ch:hardware_temporal}

\papernote{Source paper: \emph{Hardware-Based Temporal Measurements} --- \texttt{mekaneck/docs/hardware-based-temporal-measurements/}}

\begin{chapterabstract}
Consumer-grade hardware oscillators arranged in categorical coincidence networks achieve temporal resolution orders of magnitude below what quantum mechanical limits would suggest, because the measurement is categorical (frequency-domain) rather than physical (time-domain). This chapter details the hardware implementation, calibration protocol, and validation methodology. The measurements provide the empirical grounding for the trans-Planckian precision claims of Chapter~\ref{ch:molecular_maxwell} and establish the experimental infrastructure for the virtual brain validation of Chapter~\ref{ch:virtual_brain}.
\end{chapterabstract}
